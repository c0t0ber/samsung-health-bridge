# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue containing a vulnerability, OAuth details, Google Sheet identifiers, screenshots of health data, or real exported records.

Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting for this repository. Include only the minimum synthetic information needed to reproduce the problem.

## Scope

Security reports are especially welcome for:

- unintended Health Connect permissions or data access;
- OAuth scope or token-handling problems;
- writes to a spreadsheet other than the one selected by the user;
- health data written to logs or persistent local storage;
- exported records that bypass the documented privacy boundaries.
